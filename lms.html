Chapter 1: What is an online degree programme?

An online degree programme is a full degree programme that a university delivers mainly through digital systems instead of a physical classroom. The learner logs in to the university's learning platform to watch lectures, read study material, submit assignments, take assessments and ask for help.

Why this module matters

Almost every learner begins with the same question: "How does an online degree actually work?" Your answer decides whether the learner trusts the rest of the conversation. This module gives you the knowledge behind that answer, so you can explain it in your own words rather than from a script.

The Four-Quadrant Model

Remember it as Watch, Read, Ask, Test: Quadrant I e-Tutorial, Quadrant II e-Content, Quadrant III Discussion, Quadrant IV Assessment. Use the idea in simple words with a learner; the label is for you.

⚡ The One-Minute Explanation
  1. Name the three modes (Regular, ODL/Distance, Online).
  2. Give one line on each (Campus vs Self-study + centres vs Full digital LMS).
  3. Link to the learner's schedule (Working professional flexibility).
  4. Suggest a clear next step (Review syllabus & eligibility).
Golden Rule: Never describe ODL/Distance and Online as the same thing, and never promise exam/live-class formats without checking current university regulations.
